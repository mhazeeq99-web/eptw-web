## About ePTW

**ePTW is a digital Permit to Work platform designed to simplify how organisations manage work permits, approvals and safety documentation.**

Developed by **Movique Services**, ePTW brings the traditional Permit to Work process into a centralised digital platform, helping EHS and Operations teams manage permits more efficiently while maintaining clear records and accountability.

## Our Purpose

Permit to Work processes often involve paperwork, manual approvals, multiple stakeholders and difficulty tracking permit status.

ePTW was created to provide a simpler digital workflow where teams can:

- Create and manage work permits
- Review and approve permits digitally
- Manage contractor work activities
- Complete JHA / JSA and HIRARC documentation
- Record LOTO and gas testing requirements
- Track permit status and history
- Maintain a clear audit trail
- Access permits from desktop and mobile devices

## Built for Modern Workplaces

ePTW is designed with **EHS, HSE, Safety and Operations teams** in mind.

Our goal is to make Permit to Work management easier to adopt without unnecessarily complicating the workflow. The platform focuses on the practical needs of organisations managing routine and high-risk work activities.

## Our Approach

We believe digital safety systems should be:

**Simple. Practical. Traceable.**

A Permit to Work system should help people understand what work is being performed, what hazards are involved, what controls are required, who has reviewed the work, and when the permit is closed.

ePTW brings these elements together in one digital workflow.
